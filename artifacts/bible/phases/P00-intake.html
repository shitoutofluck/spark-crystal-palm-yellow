Habit — m-P00-drawings-before-permit

ORNL wants plans and specs, a bid package, a contract that points at the blueprints, and then the building permit. CMHC wants drawings to scale so a plans examiner can tell if the house is code-compliant, and a competent designer on that set. Foundation type and the radon stub belong on those drawings now. Do not move dirt first.

Do not copy CMHC plumbing/heating/electrical/health-unit permits as a US habit. Do not invent custom vs spec vs ADU as competing Methods. Not a stamp. Not legal advice.

Pass / fail / save (book hold points)

Book hold points, not a stamp, not an AHJ fail list. Full rollup on Inspectors.

LOOKS AT: zoning before planning; drawings to scale; contract references blueprints and specs with an excavation allowance; building permit acquired.

DO NOT CHEAP: drawings and specs before the permit; excavation allowance in the contract; foundation type and radon stub on the drawings now.

SMART SAVE: pre-construction duration is variable — do not invent a schedule. Late swap of foundation type after permit is a type change. Catch on the drawings.

Mess-ups: Gap until the book names them. Delivery-method alts: Gap (gap-P00-delivery-method-alts).
